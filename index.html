About Shane

Shane Barnard is a sales and hospitality leader based in the Guntersville / Huntsville area of North Alabama, with more than 15 years of experience in lodging, group and banquet sales, and full-cycle B2B selling. He has led teams of 20+, including commission-based sales teams, and has run the sales meetings that keep those teams focused on pipeline, forecasting and closing.

His hospitality background spans hotel sales at Hilton and Embassy Suites properties and hands-on operations at a full-service Holiday Inn, including restaurant, bar and banquet operations. As a founder and co-founder of two companies, he has owned everything from prospecting and proposals to budgeting and P&L.

Shane is open to sales, sales management, operations and hospitality leadership roles in North Alabama.
